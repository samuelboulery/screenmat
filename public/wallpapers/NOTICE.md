# Wallpapers

None of the images in this folder are covered by the MIT licence that applies to
the rest of this repository. They are included so that a screenshot can be shown
on the desktop it was taken on. All of them are resized and re-encoded as WebP
by `docs/assets/wallpapers.ts`.

If you hold rights to any of these images and want them removed, open an issue
at <https://github.com/samuelboulery/screenmat/issues> and they will be taken
down.

## macOS

The default desktop pictures of macOS 11 to 27 (Big Sur, Monterey, Ventura,
Sonoma, Sequoia, Tahoe, Golden Gate), resized to 3840 × 2400.

**They are © Apple Inc.** screenmat is not affiliated with Apple.

Originals: <https://512pixels.net/projects/default-mac-wallpapers-in-5k/>

## Windows

The default desktop pictures of Windows 11 (Bloom, light and dark), Windows 10
(Hero), Windows 8, Windows 7 (Harmony) and Windows XP (Bliss, photographed by
Charles O'Rear), files named `windows-*`. Only Bloom exists at 3840 × 2400; the
others are kept at the size they were published in — 1920 × 1200, and 800 × 500
for Bliss — and are not upscaled.

**They are © Microsoft Corporation.** screenmat is not affiliated with Microsoft.

Originals: <https://windowswallpaper.miraheze.org/>
